# Copy

Visitors decide a site was generated from the words before they notice the layout. Write like a specific business talking to a specific customer.

## Zero em dashes. Zero en dashes

No `—` (em dash) and no `–` (en dash) anywhere a visitor can see: headlines, body, buttons, captions, alt text, meta descriptions, quotes, footers. Not "sparingly". Zero. HTML entities count too (`&mdash;`, `&ndash;`, `&#8212;`, `&#8211;`).

Rewrite the sentence instead of swapping the character:

| Instead of | Write |
|---|---|
| We build websites — fast. | We build websites fast. |
| Three services — design, build, launch. | Three services: design, build, launch. |
| Our team — all ex-agency — ships weekly. | Our team (all ex-agency) ships weekly. |
| It's not a template — it's yours. | Built for you, not from a template. |
| Mon–Fri, 9–5 | Mon to Fri, 9am to 5pm |
| 2019–2026 | 2019-2026 (a plain hyphen for number ranges) |
| "Great work." — Priya R. | "Great work." then the name on its own line |

Also swapped for a plain hyphen or removed: ` -- ` double hyphens used as dashes.

## Words that mark copy as generated

Delete on sight. Replace with the concrete thing you mean.

**Verbs:** elevate, unleash, unlock, empower, supercharge, revolutionize, transform (as a vague verb), leverage, harness, streamline, delve, embark, foster, spearhead, reimagine, redefine, turbocharge, skyrocket.

**Adjectives:** seamless, cutting-edge, next-gen, next-level, state-of-the-art, world-class, best-in-class, game-changing, innovative, robust, bespoke (unless it is tailoring), holistic, dynamic, synergistic, curated (unless it is a museum), unparalleled, unmatched, top-notch, stunning, breathtaking.

**Nouns and phrases:** journey, tapestry, realm, landscape (for markets), testament, game-changer, synergy, "in today's fast-paced world", "in the digital age", "look no further", "take it to the next level", "we're passionate about", "your one-stop shop", "the power of", "at the intersection of", "more than just", "whether you're X or Y", "we've got you covered".

## Sentence patterns that mark copy as generated

- **"Not just X, it's Y"** and **"It's not X. It's Y."** The single most common AI construction.
- **Three-word fragments.** "Fast. Simple. Powerful." Once on a whole site at most, never as the hero.
- **Rule of three everywhere.** Every list having exactly three items with the same rhythm.
- **Rhetorical question openers.** "Tired of slow websites?" "Ready to grow?"
- **Colon titles.** "Design: Reimagined." "The Future of X: Here."
- **Emoji in headlines, buttons, or bullets.** Use real icons or nothing.

## What good copy does instead

- **Headline:** the outcome for the customer, in their words, 4 to 9 words. "Emergency plumbing in Leeds, at your door in 60 minutes" beats "Elevate your plumbing experience".
- **Subtext:** one sentence, under 20 words, that answers "how" or "for whom".
- **Buttons:** 1 to 3 words, verb first. "Book a table", "Get a quote", "Start free".
- **One label per intent.** If the nav says "Book a call", the hero and footer say "Book a call" too, not "Get in touch" and "Let's talk".
- **Numbers are specific.** "4,380 homes wired since 2011" beats "thousands of happy customers". Never "99.9%" or "10x" unless measured.
- **Proper nouns over categories.** The street, the product name, the certification body, the neighborhood.

## Honesty rules

- **Never invent testimonials, reviews, client logos, stats, awards, or certifications.** For a client site without real ones yet, use a clearly marked placeholder the owner must replace, like `[Customer quote: name, suburb]`, and tell the user which ones need real content.
- **No placeholder people.** No "John Doe", "Jane Smith", "Acme Inc", `lorem ipsum`, `example@example.com`, `(555) 123-4567` left in shipped pages.
- **No fake product UI.** Do not build a pretend dashboard out of divs to look like a screenshot. Use a real screenshot or leave it out.

## Labels and section headings

- **Eyebrows** (small uppercase labels above a heading) at most once every three sections. On every section they turn into a template rhythm.
- No section numbers as decoration ("01 / Services", "002 . Process").
- No "Scroll to explore", no "Crafted with care", no "Made with love".
- Headings name the thing plainly: "Pricing", "How it works", "Work". Cleverness goes in the headline, not the label.

## Final pass

Read the page out loud as the business owner. Anything you would feel silly saying to a customer's face gets cut.
